About Zetetikos

Education that invites you to ask better questions.

Zetetikos is an education and language-training initiative built around purposeful learning, communication and intellectual curiosity. The name evokes inquiry: the habit of asking, exploring and refining our understanding.

Our focus is practical and personal: understand what you need, design a sensible path and work toward progress you can use beyond the lesson. Services are delivered online, with programs discussed and adapted to each enquiry.
